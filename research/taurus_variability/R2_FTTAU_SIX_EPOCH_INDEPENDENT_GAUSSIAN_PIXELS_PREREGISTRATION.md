# Taurus R2 — preregistered original-image pixel-residual audit for six frozen FT Tau epochs

**12 November 2026. This protocol is committed before the first real original-data execution of this increment.**

## Fixed sources and nonclaims

The scientific question is whether the three preselected **late-night, native ZTF PSF chi below 0.5** candidate sources show obviously different **independently fitted original science-image pixel residuals** from their own three preselected *early-night* high-chi counterparts. The same physical FT Tau source has not been authenticated between original Gaia DR3 ID **149623711269425408** and ZTF detections. The analytic Gaussian fitted here is **not** the ZTF production native PSF; the original images share their reduction pipeline and therefore this is not an independent instrument calibration.

Exactly six already-frozen original date/filter/field/OID tuples from PR #46's original-data run 43275404347 are used. They are the early and late exposures of (g,1605), (g,609), and (r,609), selected before looking at chi; the (r,1605) stratum remains explicitly excluded. The exact six pre-existing full native PSFcat SHA-256, original MJD and 14-digit `filefracday` values must match the permanent PR #46-derived summary and previous PR #44/45 original summary. An epoch/PSF-hash mismatch **fails the entire study closed**; do not replace observations or source candidates. Gaia original exact-ID five-parameter astrometry must be retrieved and propagated using the existing approximate geocentric/parallax solution. Do not merge any ZTF OID source tables or light curves.

## Frozen independent fixed-pixel diagnostic

For each **exact same six dated original exposures** (without source quality selection), re-fetch original full `psfcat.fits` to verify provenance, then fetch the **original IRSA 60-arcsec ZTF science FITS cutout** and **same-dated 60-arcsec mask FITS cutout**, centred on the epoch-propagated Gaia position. Inspect original science/mask pixel/WCS agreement with existing independent repo mask QA before fitting.

- Use original 2D celestial WCS to centre a **4.5-pixel fixed radius circular core** around the epoch-propagated Gaia point (not the brightest selected centre). Require ≥40 finite, original BAD_MASK_BITS=6141-free core pixels and at least 75 usable pixels in the **9–15-pixel annulus**.
- The local sky background is the **median** of original annulus pixels, excluding source-mask BAD bits/nonfinite values. The scale is the **1.4826×median absolute deviation** of those sky pixels. If the sky noise is zero, nonfinite or otherwise invalid, or if an image or mask is unavailable, record **unresolved instrumental QA** and do not substitute other frames.
- On those fixed core pixels fit **a positive-amplitude circular analytic Gaussian with four fitted parameters: amplitude, x centre, y centre, sigma**, with sky median fixed. Start the centre at the pixel with maximum positive sky-subtracted signal within 2.5 pixels of the propagated Gaia position, then constrain the centre to within **2 pixels** of Gaia. Constrain pixel Gaussian sigma to **[0.6, 4.5] pixels**. Use bounded robust scipy least-squares `soft_l1`, `f_scale=sky MAD`, max 300 evaluations. Require finite positive initial peak/noise, a successful fit and non-boundary parameters; if not, leave a clear failure label, never reuse a different source or adjust cutoffs after observing data.
- Without using native ZTF chi in the pixel fit, report **derived** background-noise-normalized **core RMS residual**, **median absolute residual**, source Gaussian FWHM in arcsec and relative fitted centroid-to-propagated-Gaia arcsec (all clearly labelled *proxy/heuristic*; not the instrument's reduced chi-square, source-specific covariance, native PSF fitting, independent WCS errors or proper motion). Persist original full native PSFcat SHA, science+mask cutout SHA, QA status, approximate pixel-scale and diagnostic only, never pixels, raw source catalogue rows, flux, photometry or source IDs.
- All six frames are retrieved and reported even if original native source passes vs fails differ. Per stratum, describe both early- and late-epoch ratios **only when both fits are usable**; a difference cannot be interpreted as time variability due to differing seeing/conditions/models. The prior original 5/8 acceptance, 3/8 rejection, and 1/4 acceptable two-night pairs remain unchanged.

## Acceptance limits

Instrumental acceptance = independently reproducible fixed-image numerical diagnostics with original-file checksums and explicit shortcomings; not discovery or improvement of the physical FT Tau target quality. Do **not** change the old chi≥0.5 target gate or reclassify any of three native PSF rejects. Original reference controls from PR #46 were *same-pipeline* and are **not** independent stellar truth. No high-resolution deblending, independent original PSF/WCS covariance, physical Gaia–ZTF association, significant stellar motion or astrophysical variability/companions is established here.

Original archive: https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/program_interface/ztf_api.html. Frozen existing methods: `v15_image_pixels`, `v113_official_masks_psf`, `r2_fttau_chi_reference_controls`, `v19_gaia_covariance`.
